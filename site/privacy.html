Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

This site is a plain list of addons. There are no accounts, no comment boxes and no sign-up forms.

What we collect

The site itself does not store personal data. Our host (Netlify) and file host (GitHub) keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type to run and protect their services. We do not receive or use those logs for tracking.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser session storage only to cache the addon list so pages load faster. It stays on your device and is cleared when you close the tab. We do not set tracking cookies.

Third parties

  • Fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Addon files and images are served from GitHub.
